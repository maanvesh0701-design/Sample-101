FAQ

Do I need internet access to use Shubhrali?

No. Once you have downloaded the APK and the model weights, Shubhrali runs 100% locally on your device's native silicon. No cloud connection is required for inference.

What is the difference between the MVP and the final build?

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) is designed purely to test the core thesis of on-device processing. Future versions will integrate the broader civic super-app features including community health resources.

Is my conversation data stored anywhere?

Your conversation data never leaves your device. Because Shubhrali is offline-first, there are no external servers, no cloud telemetry, and no ad-targeting mechanisms involved.

How do I load the GGUF weights into the app?

After installing the APK, open the Shubhrali app, navigate to settings, and select the local path where you saved the 1.12 GB Qwen GGUF file. The app will automatically initialize the local neural engine.

Are all features available on standard Android devices?

Yes, provided your device has sufficient RAM to load a 1.5B parameter model (typically 4GB+ RAM recommended). Performance scales dynamically with your phone's processor.
